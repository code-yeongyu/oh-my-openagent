# Model and sources

The Mobius Loop connects three repeatable areas of work:

- **Discover:** understand the situation, affected people, desired change, and
  how progress could be observed.
- **Decide:** weigh alternatives using what has been learned and choose a route
  toward the intended outcomes.
- **Deliver:** act on the selected route, gather feedback, and evaluate impact.

**Foundation** sustains culture, collaboration, and technical capability across
all three. It is continuing support rather than a final phase. Open Practice
Library also uses the names Discovery, Options, and Delivery; its Decide link
opens the Options category.

An output is something produced. An outcome is the change sought. A diagnostic
command is an output; a measured reduction in diagnosis time is an outcome.
Evidence may justify another delivery, a different option, or renewed discovery.
A bounded initiative can end when its outcomes are achieved or its purpose
changes.

The SDK's schemas, revision checks, provenance categories, pause behavior, and
closure rules are OmO implementation choices. Open Practice Library supplies a
model and optional practices, not a mandatory software transition graph.
