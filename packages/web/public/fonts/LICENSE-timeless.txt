1
Introduction

We give the Timeless type family away free of charge so that anyone can set their work in it, personal or commercial. It is not open source: the fonts may not be modified or renamed, and they travel only as part of a work, never on their own. That way every copy stays the same, and stays free.

This contract lets you use, embed and build with the fonts in any medium, and pass them on with your work. It asks only that you do not sell them, alter them, redistribute them on their own or use them to train AI, and that you send anyone who wants just the fonts to timeless.co.

2
Free and perpetual, with nothing to count

The license is free and it is perpetual. There are no fees, no subscriptions and no tiers. You never have to count users, devices, company size, impressions, page views or projects, because none of them are limited.

We cannot withdraw the license on a whim. It ends only if you break this contract (section 8.1).

3
Definitions

"We" and "us" are Timeless Ventures Private Limited, Chennai, the design studio that publishes the fonts and holds the copyright in them.

"You" are any person or organisation that obtains a copy of the fonts.

"The fonts" are the font files we release under this license, together with the stylesheets, build files and documentation that come with them.

A "custom cut" is a set of fonts generated by our configurator at timeless.co/type/playground, including static instances and variable fonts with alternates baked in.

An "embedding conversion" is subsetting, hinting, compressing or converting the fonts to another font format (such as WOFF or WOFF2) solely so that they can be delivered as part of a work.

A "work" is any document, website, application, template, theme, starter kit, software package, video, product, artwork or other output that is set in or uses the fonts.

The "Timeless name" is "Timeless" and any font name that begins with or contains it.

What you can do
5.1
Any use, any medium

You may use the fonts for personal and commercial purposes in any medium, including print, web, applications, video, games, packaging, signage, products and marks.

5.2
Logos and marks

You may use the fonts in a logo or mark, and you may convert text set in the fonts to outlines within a specific work, such as a logo, illustration or print-ready file. Doing so gives you no right in the typeface design itself, and you may not claim exclusive rights in its letterforms as such.

5.3
Embedding and distributing with your work

You may embed the fonts in websites, web, mobile and desktop apps, documents, PDFs, ebooks, games and video, and make embedding conversions to do so. You may distribute them as part of a work, free or paid, such as in its code repository (public or private), a template or theme, or an npm package (see 5.5), and edit the stylesheets and build files to suit the work.

5.4
Custom cuts

A custom cut is covered by this license and may be used on the same terms. Apart from embedding conversions (5.3), the configurator is the only permitted way to produce a variant of the fonts.

5.5
Sharing the font files

You may share the fonts with collaborators and clients working on the same work. Whenever you pass on the font files, to them or in a repository, template or package, include a copy of this contract; anyone who receives them may use the fonts only on these terms. Nobody needs to buy anything. A website, app or document that simply uses the fonts needs no copy.

5.6
Your work is yours

Works created with the fonts are not subject to this license and remain entirely yours. No attribution is required.

6
Restrictions

Now that you know what you can do, here is what you can't do:

Sell, sublicense, rent or lend the fonts themselves. Selling a work that includes them is fine (5.3)
Redistribute the fonts on their own, in whole or in part, whether original, custom cut or embedding conversion: on a font service or download site, in a font collection, or in a repository, package or other project whose sole purpose is to distribute them
Pass on the font files without a copy of this contract (5.5)
Modify the font files by adding to, deleting from or substituting any of their components, by changing formats or by porting them to a new environment, other than through a custom cut or an embedding conversion
Decompile or reverse engineer the fonts, or extract their outlines to make another font
Make new fonts based on the design that is in the fonts
Rename the fonts, or use the Timeless name to name any font, product or service
Use the fonts or their designs to train or fine-tune an AI model, or add them to a training dataset. Using AI tools to make a work set in the fonts is fine
