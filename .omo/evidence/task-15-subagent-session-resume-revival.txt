Chaos regression found and fixed during GREEN (this is why the bench exists):
 iter 93 seed=3109004448: inv1 parent enqueued 2 times for (task:epoch) st_01a3185c:0
 Root cause traced via deterministic replay: a completion BUFFERED in-memory behind a transient
 parent state (notified_epoch still -1) was delivered by reconcileUnnotifiedNotifications AND
 again by the later flushBuffered - the flush owns delivery of a live buffered (task_id, run_epoch).
 Fix: reconcile skips records whose (task_id, run_epoch) is still held in the in-memory buffer;
 it only recovers notifications whose buffer died with the process. Covered by new unit test
 "#given a completion buffered behind a transient parent state ... reconcile skips it and the
 flush delivers exactly once". Replay after fix: violations: [].
