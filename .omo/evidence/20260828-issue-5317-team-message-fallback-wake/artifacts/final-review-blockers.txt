Observed behavior
-----------------

- Permanently failing queue revalidation is dropped after three attempts and
  the next queued prompt dispatches.
- Exact unread lookup recognizes a message while it is held in the
  `.delivering-<messageId>.json` reservation slot.
- Async revalidation uses its own five-second production cap.
- Retry classification retains the queue entry without clearing semantic
  dedupe or the post-dispatch reservation hold.
- Non-retryable fallback failures remain covered separately and do not retry.
- Mailbox fallback wakes use capped exponential backoff beyond the global
  three-failure limit, rotate behind unrelated prompts, and remain queued
  until dispatch succeeds or exact-message revalidation cancels them.
- Definite connect-phase failures include network/host unreachable, network
  down, unavailable local address, and Undici connect timeout codes.
