SUMMARY
-------
Extended the residency->label mapping in three surface areas so that
persisted_only/rpc_detached children render as "suspended":

1. status-row-format.ts (omo-senpi): formatTaskRow, buildWidgetRows,
   backgroundWidgetRows, and compactTaskContext all use a shared
   statusLabel() helper that maps persisted_only/rpc_detached to
   "suspended" instead of the raw status (e.g. "running").

2. renderers.ts (senpi-task tools/output): the task_output status row
   uses "suspended" as the status label when the snapshot carries
   suspended details.

3. snapshot.ts (senpi-task tools/output): buildTaskSnapshot now
   carries residency_state from the record and attaches a
   SuspendedDetails explanation when the residency is persisted_only
   or rpc_detached. statusText() in output.ts surfaces this
   explanation in the model-facing status text.
