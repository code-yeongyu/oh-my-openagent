The normal migration paths all pass. I am nevertheless rejecting the literal claim that **any marker-bearing legacy `omo` wrapper is deleted**: when `bin/omo` is a symlink to a marker-bearing wrapper, the installer exits 0 but leaves the symlink and target in place. The concurrency failure is an additional robustness defect, though not specific to the rename.
