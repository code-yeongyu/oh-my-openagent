## 2026-11-27 - Bound foreground preview delivery to the current lease

Both offering and draining a picture require an active subscriber, the foreground lease, enabled preview, and known unlocked and non-secure state. A stopped or replaced lease cannot replay queued frames. No background viewer authority or wire method was added.

The session grant module owns preview admission and polls the stop listener again at delivery. Tests exercise pending-frame invalidation through release, revoke, display release, `stopPath.stop`, and a queued stop chord.
