### Fixed

- A `thread_send` to a busy thread on another device that was queued there as a follow-up now answers `queued` with its `delivery_id` and `effective_mode`. It used to answer `idempotency_uncertain` ("accepted the message but did not report where it waits") even though the message was delivered and answered. The receiving engine's `queued` admission is its receipt, and its pending-message count does not include admitted deliveries. ([#14013](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14013), fixes [#14012](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/14012))
