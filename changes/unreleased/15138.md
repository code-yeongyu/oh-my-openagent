## 2026-11-27 - Stranded children: refused warm revival, reused owner pid, paused orphans (#12077, #12080)

A refused warm revival restores the finished record instead of leaving it `running` with no outcome tracker. Host claims carry a start-identity stamp so a reused pid is told from the live claimant; records written before this keep the signal-0 check. A child paused by its own run defers as `run_paused`, and reconcile parks a paused orphan for its run. Port of #12078 and #12086.
