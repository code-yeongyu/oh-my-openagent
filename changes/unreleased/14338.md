## 2026-11-19 - Hand session-of-pid to the engine unchanged (#14338)

`packages/omo-native/bin/lib/engine-commands.js` adds `session-of-pid` to `ENGINE_COMMANDS`, so the compiled entry (`compile-args.ts` `buildSenpiArgs`) and the npm launcher pass `session-of-pid <pid> --json` to the engine as is. Before, the launcher put `--extension <plugin>` in front of it and the engine failed with `Unknown option: --json`.
