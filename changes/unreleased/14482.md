## 2026-11-20 - Apply thinking suffixes when switching thread models (#14482)

`thread_set_model` matches against the target catalog and applies a recognized thinking suffix to the target session after the model switch. An unsupported level returns `thinking_level_unsupported`. If the requested provider has no available models on the target, `model_not_found` includes `provider_not_configured` and the available providers.
