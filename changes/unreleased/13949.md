### Fixed

- When a session ends, it now closes the connection to this device's OmO device service that it opened for reaching other devices. `omo remote open`, `send`, `model`, `interrupt`, `close` and `attach` now also release what they used to reach the other device once they are done. Before, an idle hold on the other device's session could stay open for up to 10 minutes after the session or command had ended.
