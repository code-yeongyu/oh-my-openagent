## 2026-11-09 - An uncertain probe is host_unavailable and leaves no receipt (#12947, fixes #12925, part of #12879)

`thread/remote/relay-transport.ts`: the read-only `get_state` probe before a steer maps an `uncertain` outcome to `host_unavailable` (`probeFailure`); other probe refusals keep `outcomeFailure`. `once()` hands `send` a `sending` callback that runs right before the `admit_external_message` record is sent; a call that returns or throws before it drops its `prepared` receipt through the new store op `toolReceiptRelease` (`gateway/store-relay-ops.ts`: deletes only this instance's `prepared` row), so the next call under the same key runs again. A receipt that cannot be dropped is settled as before.
