### Changed

- A reply to a message that came from another of your devices goes to the session on that device that sent it, whether you reply by its session id or by the name it gave. A session on another device, or on this one, that only carries the same id or name no longer receives the reply. ([#13218](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13218), part of [#13037](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/13037))
