**The frontend skill now builds in ten habits that design reviewers check for.** Before designing a new screen, a redesign, or a design system, it reads the guideline source in full, defines the ideal state per persona and journey step, and records every gap in a ledger. Controls keep the same box size in every state, and a spinner or arrow never adds padding. Pointer targets are at least 24 px, colour roles come before components (built from shadcn/ui anatomy), glass and dated trend surfaces stay out unless the brief asks for them, and the subtract pass counts the controls. Every UI or design-system deliverable states a per-criterion WCAG 2.2 AA result.
