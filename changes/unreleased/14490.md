### Fixed

- `thread_close` of a thread that an interactive terminal holds on another device now answers what a close on this device answers: `unsupported` with `retry_with: { graceful: true }` and a next step that works (close it with `graceful: true`, or interrupt its turn). Before, it told the caller to update omo on that device, even when both devices ran the current omo.
