## 2026-10-28 - One loser outcome for the reconcile ownership race (#11667)

`reconcileRecords` reported a record whose ownership changed during the liveness probe as `foreign_live_owner` / `ownership changed during probe`, while a loser that saw the live owner at the scan or lost the reclaim CAS reported `deferred` / `foreign_live_owner`. Only the deferred shape schedules the scoped retry (`retryDeferredHostSessions`), so the probe-window loser skipped it.
