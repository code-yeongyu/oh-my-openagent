### Changed

- The frontend skill now covers HTML reports, figures, diagrams, slides and Notion or Slack images, not only web UI. Its `check-accent-stripes.ts` linter fails a coloured bar along one side of a box whether the box is rounded or square, and a 2px-or-wider accent outline on a rounded card. The `visualize` skill runs the linter before previewing or showing a page (#10774).
