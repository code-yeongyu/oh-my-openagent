### Fixed

- `omo login`, `omo logout`, `omo whoami` and the `omo account` verbs no longer stop on "an unexpected error" when another process holds this computer's OmO sign-in lock. They name the holder (pid, how long it has held the lock, the lock file) and the one action that frees it. ([#14030](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14030))
- The macOS keychain helper ends when the omo command that started it exits, even if its input stays open. A keychain change it already started gets at most 30 seconds to finish, and every helper run ends after 10 minutes, so a leftover helper can no longer hold the sign-in lock for days. ([#14030](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14030))
- `omo logout` over a keychain item another program owns names the `omo-cli` item and the Keychain Access step instead of asking to unlock the keychain. ([#14030](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14030))
