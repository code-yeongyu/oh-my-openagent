**Memory sync keeps working when you sign out and back in on the desktop.** Signing out and signing in again (with the same or another account) while the engine runs used to leave Settings > Memory > Sync on "Memory sync authority is unavailable." until the engine restarted. The engine now ends only the old account when the desktop retires it, ends that account's open sync sessions once, and picks up the new account on the next status read. It needs a desktop that keeps the private channel open across the rollover; with an older desktop the behaviour is unchanged. Carried by the next train top's bundle regeneration.
