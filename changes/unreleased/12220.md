### Fixed

- On Fedora, RHEL and other systems whose CA bundle carries non-ASCII comment lines between certificates (such as `# NetLock Arany (Class Gold) Főtanúsítvány`), the compiled engine now imports the system roots instead of discarding the whole bundle as malformed and falling back. Non-ASCII text inside a certificate block still discards the file. ([#12220](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/12220), part of [#10762](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/10762))
