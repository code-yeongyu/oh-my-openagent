### Added

- thread: a thread knows who opened it. `thread_create` answers `created_by` (null when no session made the call), `thread_list` rows and `thread_read` show `created_by`, and `thread_list` takes `children_of` to list only the threads one thread opened; the device that hosts a new thread records its opener too, so the answer holds on both devices and after a restart (#15221)
