### Fixed

- A thread tool given the bare id of a closed thread on another device (`thread_create({ thread })`, `thread_send`, `thread_read`, the control tools) reaches it as `<device>/<thread>` does. When every device is asked at once, a device that answers its own lookup in time could pass the 3.5 s cap and be counted silent, so the call answered `host_unavailable`. Now, when that first round finds no holder, each device that stayed silent is asked again alone, one at a time, with no other lookup of that device service in flight. A retry is made only when the earlier lookups finish in time and the call has enough of its time budget left, so `host_unavailable` can still name a device that was not asked again, as well as one that did not answer on its own.
