### Fixed

- A memory edit reaches your other signed-in devices sooner. The device that made the edit now sends it 300 ms after the commit instead of 2 s, and skips a check with the sync service when nothing new has been announced. The receiving device skips one more request when the service has already said what changed. When the update stream is refused, sync keeps checking every 10 s instead of going quiet, and the reason is logged. A stream the service closes on schedule reconnects at once without missing an update. ([#14493](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14493), part of [#14476](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/14476))
