### Changed

- `thread_read` of a thread on another device no longer downloads the whole transcript. The other device sends only its newest session entries in pages, and this device keeps them, so a repeat read of an unchanged thread costs one empty page. Measured on a long desktop session with `max_bytes: 1500`: 12.5 s before, 2.3 s on a fresh connection and 0.47 s on a held one after. `next_cursor` and `cursor_stale` work as before; a device whose engine cannot page keeps the old full read.

## 2026-11-17 - Paged thread_read for threads on other devices (#14053)

- `src/components/thread/remote/transcript-tail.ts`: `readEntryWindow` reads the newest entries with `get_entries` (`tail`, then `before`, up to 256 entries / 512 KiB per page) until 60 messages are held, keeps the window per (port, device, session), and refreshes it with `since`. A refused page marks the device unpaged for the port.
- `src/components/thread/paged-reader.ts`: `selectEntryPage` applies the live reader's window and byte budget to the message entries; entry cursors bind the `leafId.newest` revision.
- `src/components/thread/tools/read-ops.ts`: `readLive` tries the control's `readEntryPage` before `getMessages`.
- `src/components/thread/read-budget.ts`: the shared byte-budget rule, moved out of `reader.ts`.
- `src/components/remote/port/device-service.ts`: `get_entries` is a read-only record (failures are typed refusals).
