## 2026-10-28 - Keep the limiter lease with a self-resumed run (#11685)

A child resuming on its own after its host reconciled the turn as interrupted re-armed outcome tracking under the next run epoch while the earlier run's lease was still held, so nothing released that lease and each reconnect-and-resume leaked one slot until `global_concurrency` stayed full with no live child (#11677). The revive reservation now moves a still-held lease to the new run, as a re-dispatch does, and acquires only when none is held. `TaskManager.leaseHolders()` reports each lease with its task's status, residency, current run and whether a live child is behind it.

## 2026-10-28 - List task limiter lease holders (#11685)
