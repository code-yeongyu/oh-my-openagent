### Added

- Thread tools take a thread's account-wide alias and a `device` to create a thread on, the groundwork for reaching your other signed-in devices: every call answers exactly as it would on this device, and a Free account gets a clear error. Reaching another device arrives with the device-service wiring that follows. A new `thread_close` closes a session on this device or on another one; a thread with no live session answers `closed: false`, and a terminal's own session stays with its terminal. ([#12335](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/12335), part of [#12320](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/12320))
