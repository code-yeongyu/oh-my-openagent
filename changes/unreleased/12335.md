### Added

- Thread tools can reach sessions on your other signed-in devices: open or fork a thread on another device by its id or alias, create one there with `device`, list them all with `all_scope`, and read, message or interrupt them like local ones. Thread aliases are account-wide. Reaching another device needs a paid plan; a Free account gets a clear error and its local threads work as before. ([#12335](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/12335), part of [#12320](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/12320))
