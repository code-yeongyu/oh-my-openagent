### Changed

- The SDK's principal matrix covers the five rows revision 22 of the security design adds: the send idempotency and turn-guard rules, account.devices, account.show and account.signInCheck, and the conditions on install.setup.apply. On the SDK root, which is not released yet, this changes four answers: config.status is refused to a session and to a service, and account.show and account.signInCheck to a session, with sender_not_allowed (reason "held") until the slices that meet their conditions; and account.devices, refused to a connector and to a service, now carries the matrix's refusal instead of the no-cell default (no "no_matrix_cell" reason). No shipped command or tool changes behaviour.
