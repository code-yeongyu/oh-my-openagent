### Fixed

- A task child started as its own process no longer fails with `chain_not_held: timeout` when its engine takes longer than 10 s to boot, as on a cold Windows runner. The wait for the engine's first answer now ends only when the engine stops making CPU progress for 10 s, uses more than 120 s of CPU, or reaches a 10 minute outer limit; the 10 s limit now covers only the fallback-chain exchange itself. ([#14808](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14808), fixes [#13165](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/13165))
