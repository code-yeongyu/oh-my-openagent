**Each finished background task reports to its parent with its answer.** A parent used to hear only when all of its background tasks were done, with no results in the notice, and then spent a `background_output` call per child. Every finished task now sends its own `<task-notification>` with the child's final answer inline, and `task` takes `wake: "none"` to keep the notice for the parent's next turn instead of starting one.
