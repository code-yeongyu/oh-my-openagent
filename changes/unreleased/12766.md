### Fixed

- When another device refuses a `thread_send` or `thread_handoff` because the chain went past that device user's thread chain limit (`threads.chainCountLimit`), the tool now answers `chain_count_limit`, naming the device and the setting, instead of `unsupported` with advice to update omo on that device. A queued delivery to a session there that is not running maps a chain-limit refusal from that device's gateway store to the same code, with the chain count and the limit, and the receiving consumer now passes on the store's own refusal reason (for example `sender_not_allowed`) instead of the coarse code.
