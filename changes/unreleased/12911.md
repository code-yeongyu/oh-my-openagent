**`omo remote send` now travels the same road as a session's `thread_send` to another device, and every reply from another device is explained by one mapping.** The person at a terminal who sends to a session on another device goes through the relay transport (the same offline check, the same steer preflight against the running turn, the same receipt dedupe) while keeping their own prompt record; what the user reads when a command on another device is refused (`omo remote`, the attach TUI, the thread tools and the SDK) comes from a single module, so a device that did not answer, a missing grant or an uncertain result reads the same everywhere. ## 2026-11-09 - omo remote send over the transport port, one outcome mapping (#12911, part of #12879)

`thread/remote/outcome.ts` holds the one mapping from a device service `CommandResult` to a `RemoteCommandOutcome` (`outcomeOf`, moved from `device-service.ts`) and from a non-ok outcome to a `ThreadToolFailure` (`outcomeFailure` and `READ_UNCERTAIN`, moved from `remote-ops.ts`); `relay-transport.ts`, `remote-controls.ts`, `remote-ops.ts`, `live-surface.ts`, `lazy-ops.ts` and omo-native's `remote-attach.ts` (the attach TUI's `command` passthrough) read it from there. `GatewaySender` gains `{ kind: "person", uid, user }` (`gateway/types.ts`; `envelope.ts` `senderFacts` keys its receipt as `person:<uid>`). `relay-transport.ts` delivers a person sender as the engine's own `prompt` record (`followUp`, or `steer` after one `get_state` against `expected_turn_id`) behind the same `deviceOnline` preflight and the same `once` receipt as a peer delivery; `local-transport.ts` refuses a person sender (`unsupported`: a person reaches a session on this device through its TUI). `omo-native/remote-session-verbs.ts` `send` builds `createRelayTransport` over this device's gateway store and delivers through it instead of `promptRemote`.
