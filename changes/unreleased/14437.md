### Added

- `omo sync status` (and `--json`) shows this device's memory sync state: the signed-in account, whether this device has joined and whether its sync switch is on, the account's devices with their sync membership and last-seen time, pending join requests, and the running engine's sync state (state, reason, last sync, held files, updates after an offline gap), or that no engine is running. It is read-only and prints no key material. ([#14437](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14437), fixes [#14435](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/14435))
