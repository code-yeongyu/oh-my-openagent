### Fixed

- Memory sync: every sign-out now retires the trust pin of the account it signs out of, so `omo account show --account <id>` no longer answers `joined` once that sign-in's keys are gone. Plain `omo account forget` (the desktop's sign-out of a CLI-item session) used to retire nothing. It now reads the stored sign-in to name its account first. A stored sign-in that cannot be read is still deleted, and its pin is left with one warning line (#12308, #12384). ([#13642](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13642))
- Memory sync: a sign-in that earned its pin before `omo sync` recorded its account now has the account read from the service on a refused refresh, plain `forget`, `forget --device` and `adopt --replace`, as `omo logout` already did, so its pin is retired too. Nothing is asked unless a live pin exists (#12383). ([#13642](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13642))
