## 2026-11-08 - Side panel child spend survives row eviction (#12747)

The side-panel store keeps at most `AGENT_ROW_CAP` child rows, but `refreshChildren` upserts every task record on every refresh. An evicted finished child looked new, so its cost was added again each time. Spend now lives in a per-child map outside the capped rows, and an evicted row stays the previous state of its child: an unchanged record neither recounts nor reports a change, and a revised cost replaces the earlier one.
