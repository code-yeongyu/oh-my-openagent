### Fixed

- A parent's Stop no longer pauses background children that another live task host still holds. Before, a parent reloaded into a new process wrote those children as `paused` while their owner kept running them, and the Resume then filed them as `lost` ("still held by live host"), so they looked dead and were cancelled while still working. Such a child is now left to its owner. A child an older build already paused under a live owner comes out of the Resume as `owned_elsewhere`, is never filed as lost or ended, and a later Resume or session start retries it once its owner is gone. (part of [#12349](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/12349))
