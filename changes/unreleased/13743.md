### Added

- An agent can be woken by a session on another device of your account: `monitor({ type: "rpc", target: "<device>/<session>" })` reports its status changes and turn ends as they happen, and after a relay drop, a device-service restart or an engine restart it reports what changed meanwhile, once. `<session>` can be the thread id `thread_list` shows, an alias, or the session's key; `<device>/*` follows every session on that device. ([#13743](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13743), [#12719](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/12719))
- `thread_read` takes `wait_ms`: for a thread on another device it first waits, up to that bound, until the thread finishes its turn, then returns the transcript with how the wait ended (`settled`, `timeout`, `cancelled` or `ended`). ([#13743](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13743), [#13417](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/13417))

`remote/remote-watch.ts` (watch v2, from the closed #12725): `RemoteSessionsPort.watch(device, session, handlers, options)` follows a session across attach ends, device-service restarts and engine restarts (re-attach with 1 s to 30 s backoff, resync from a fresh `get_state`, re-locate by durable id), deduplicates by stream seq and turn id, and bounds its queue at 256 records. `remote/attach-pool.ts` refcounts the one attach per session and detaches it 600 s after its last holder. `remote/monitor-source.ts` serves senpi's `RpcRemoteSource` seam, registered by `remote/lazy-ops.ts` `registerRemoteMonitorTargets`. `control/relay-control.ts` `waitSettled` backs `thread_read` `wait_ms`; a thread on this device answers `unsupported`. `followRemote` (`omo remote send`) still ends on a gap with its reason. `device-service-core` carries optional per-record `seqs` on `session.records`.
