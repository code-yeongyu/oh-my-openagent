### Added

- Computer use v3 Windows QA: the interactive QA job records its own session with a pinned, sha256-checked ffmpeg, and runs the driver's `--all` behind a gate. The gate is green only with exactly one `SET` line, at least one pass, no failures, and every blocked scenario named with a reason. The PowerShell interop check also catches a wide or string-marshalling Win32 import hidden behind a `[return: ...]` or parameter attribute. Test and CI only; nothing user-visible changes. ([#15175](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/15175), supersedes [#13443](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13443))
