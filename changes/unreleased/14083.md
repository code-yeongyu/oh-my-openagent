### Changed

- ulw-plan and ulw-loop plans now record what each todo writes and why each dependency edge exists: the dependency matrix columns are Todo, Writes, Depends on (reason) and Blocks. ulw-plan QA scenarios drive time and failures through a fake clock or injected fault, and a retry, timeout or lock needs a named cause. The Senpi and OpenCode ulw-plan editions no longer tell the planner to back off between waits by doubling a timeout; it keeps working until the child's completion notification arrives. ([#14083](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14083))
