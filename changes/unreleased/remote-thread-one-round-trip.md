Thread ops on another device now cost at most one relay round trip: a session's status (`get_state`) and transcript (`get_messages`, `session.read`) are answered on this device from its attach once read, until anything happens on that session; a model switch whose model is in the session's held catalog is one `set_model`; a send to an idle session is one `prompt`. A create whose reply names the session's listing key (`session_key`) skips `session.locate`.
