### Changed

- Senpi task and LSP tool descriptions now say what the tools do: `task_cancel` releases a finished child's resident session or queued messages, `task_send`'s 32 KB cap covers team messages only and a message can queue, `task_output`'s tail mode shares the character cap, `task_id` wins over `name`, and `lsp_find_references`, `lsp_symbols` and `lsp_diagnostics` state their 200-result cap and defaults. Text only; no behavior change. ([#12500](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/12500), fixes [#12496](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/12496))
