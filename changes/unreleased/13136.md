**A sign-in whose keys the device service did not take is no longer reported as adopted.** When the computer's device service already holds another live device of the account, it answers the hand-off with that device and keeps it. The hand-off now reports `held_other` and logs one line naming the held device, instead of treating the new sign-in as the computer's device.
