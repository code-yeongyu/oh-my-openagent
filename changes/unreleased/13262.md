**A team-mode messaging test no longer fails on a busy host.** Six fallback-wake tests park the recipient behind a blocker prompt whose post-dispatch hold lapses after 2 s of wall clock; on a loaded shard the queued wake pruned that lapsed hold and dispatched before the test released it, so the release returned `false`. The tests now hold the blocker on a frozen `Date.now` until the explicit release. Runtime behaviour does not change.
