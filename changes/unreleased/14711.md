### Fixed

- `omo logout` no longer waits on the credential store twice when a stalled or locked keyring fails its read: it makes one store call, clears nothing, and exits 9 as store-unavailable (the same code as `omo account`). A server answer that the device was already revoked (`device_revoked`) now reports a successful logout and clears the saved sign-in instead of reporting failure.
