### Fixed

- The thread tools accept the argument names agents predict instead of refusing them (#15073). A census of every fleet machine found 192 refused calls in 77 sessions, 139 of them `thread_read` with `limit`. `thread_read` now takes `limit` (the newest N messages, 1 to 200; also `tail`, `lines`, `count`, `last`), `limit_bytes`/`bytes` for `max_bytes`, and `wait_ms: 0`. `thread_list` takes `query` and `limit`, `thread_set_model` takes `reasoning`, and `thread_create` takes `all_scope: true`. Across the family, `mode` names `delivery` on send and handoff, `message` names `prompt` and `title` names `name` on create, and `title` names `name` on rename. A required field keeps its one name (`message` on send and handoff, `level` on set_reasoning), as the SDK calls do; a call that names `text` or `reasoning` there is refused with the field it meant. A field a tool still does not take is refused, and the refusal now shows the corrected `tool.<name>({...})` call; an alias given with a different value than its field is refused naming both. The SDK parity rows, scenarios and design section 6 carry the new fields.
