**Computer use v3 on Linux: the X11 stop chord, Esc, the self set and the doctor checks.** On X11 the physical stop chord is heard through XI2 raw key events, so an app's keyboard grab cannot swallow it, while synthetic XTEST keys never trigger it. The listener arms the chord your consent settings name, and only that one. If it cannot arm that chord, or cannot hear the keyboard at all, every action is refused (`StopPathUnavailable`, reason `deaf`) and the capabilities say why (`deafReason`). Esc ends the foreground lease before the next action runs. A window that belongs to the agent's own terminal or an engine process is refused as `SelfTarget`. A monitor layout change starts a new epoch. The X11 doctor checks (display, accessibility bus, uinput access, compositor) are reported to `diagnose` as ok, missing or unknown; `omo doctor computer` prints them once it is wired to that call. ([#13357](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13357))
