### Added

- omo Native eval cells can reach this user's machines through the bunshin TypeScript SDK: the plugin bundles `@sisyphuslabs/bunshin-ts` and `@sisyphuslabs/bunshin-ts-relay` (vendored build output pinned per file, not npm dependencies) into `runtime/bunshin-ts/sdk.js`, publishes its directory as `OMO_BUNSHIN_TS_SDK_ROOT` at activation, and the installer requires it. `pickTransport` (`BUNSHIN_TS_TRANSPORT` = `auto`, `relay` or `hub`) uses the local device service when it reports connected; this build bundles no hub transport. Over the relay, one device that is slow, offline or refused on `machine.describe` no longer fails `connect()`, `listMachines()` or `fanout()` for every machine: it is listed `unsupported` with `attention` naming why and after how long, fanout skips it, and the device the cell runs on is listed too (unsupported, since the service runs no machine operation on its own device). A `bunshin` skill pointer and removed-tool hint teach the import. ([#12258](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/12258), fixes [#12251](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/12251))
