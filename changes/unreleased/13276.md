### Changed

- `omo login --new-device` is gone. The OmO service keeps one device per computer, so registering another device over an unreadable stored sign-in either failed after approval or left two live devices. When the stored sign-in cannot be read, `omo login` gives one recovery: remove this computer's device on the Devices page if it is still listed, then run `omo account forget` (which also has the device service forget the device), then sign in again. When the device service holds the device, it also names that device and says the desktop app will be signed out on this computer and need a sign-in afterwards.
- When the credential store is locked, `omo login` says to unlock it and try again.
- When the device service is installed but does not answer, `omo login` names the service's leftover endpoint file and says that opening the OmO desktop app starts the service.
