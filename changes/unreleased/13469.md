### Changed

- `thread_list` rows carry a `state` (`running`, `idle`, `inactive` or `archived`) and the thread's `model` when known, instead of a per-thread live/alive flag; whether a device is reachable is on `devices` only. Archived threads are hidden unless a `state` filter asks for them (`omo thread list --state`), and a thread closed on this device stays listed as inactive instead of disappearing. `status` remains for now, derived from the state. ([#13469](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13469), fixes [#13403](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/13403))

### Added

- `thread_archive { thread, archived? }` archives or unarchives a thread on any device through the engine's session flag. ([#13469](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13469), part of [#13407](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/13407))
