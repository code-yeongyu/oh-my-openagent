**`omo remote` can open, send, switch model, interrupt and close sessions on your other devices.** `omo remote open <device> [--project P] [--model M]` starts a session there and prints `<device>/<session>`; `omo remote send <device>/<session> <text>` prints the answer as it streams in and returns when the turn settles (`--steer` steers the running turn, `--follow-up` queues behind it, `--no-wait` returns once the session took the message); `model`, `interrupt` and `close` do what they say. Each verb is the same remote op the agent's thread tools use, and `--json` prints its typed result. A steer to a session on another device now reaches it when that device's engine reports its running turn, instead of always being refused.
