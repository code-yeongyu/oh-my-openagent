### Changed

- Skills: `ulw-research` and mass research in `mass-ulw` now require every conclusion to rest on something a tool actually observed. Workers tag claims `OBSERVED:` and dead ends `PROBED:`, anything known only from memory is sent back as a lead, and mass-research verify nodes re-open each observed source. Both research editions state the rule in their opening contract, and every mass-ulw node prompt carries it in its VERIFY item.
