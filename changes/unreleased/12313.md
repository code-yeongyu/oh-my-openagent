### Added

- The headless desktop fake backend (`senpi-desktop-backend-fake`) can script window identity and identity changes, key-priming focus reads and focus-record outcomes, hit reads, a timed user-input history with the frontmost pid, an accessibility deadline and dense-tree knob, epoch events, a display change, and a stop marker. Every new hook records a sink op, every field defaults to today's behavior, and a timeline that could never fire (`nth: 0`, a press more than an hour away) is rejected at load.
