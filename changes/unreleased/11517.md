### Added

- Computer use (macOS): `win.bringToCurrentSpace()` moves a window that sits on another Space (a Mission Control desktop) into the Space you are using, without activating it or switching Spaces; a window already there is left alone. Like upstream oh-my-pi it needs no foreground-control grant, and it is audited like every other input. It refuses with `SpaceMoveDenied` before moving anything when your current Space is a fullscreen app's, and also when macOS will not move the window or your Space or focus changed meanwhile; other platforms, and macOS versions without the needed window-server functions, refuse with `SpaceUnsupported`. The engine method is `window.bringToCurrentSpace`. ([#11517](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/11517), part of [#11463](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/11463))
