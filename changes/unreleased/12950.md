**Code-review, QA and gate-review helpers can no longer change your installed agent or its settings.** They now always run as their own sandboxed process (bubblewrap on Linux, the built-in sandbox on macOS) that may only write inside the project and its own scratch space, and never in `~/.omo`, the project's `.git`, `.omo`, `.claude`, `.agents` or `.mcp.json`, or through local sockets to other running services. Where no sandbox can be started (Windows, or a Linux host that blocks unprivileged namespaces) the helper is refused with a message saying why, instead of running unconfined (senpi#6287).
