### Fixed

- `thread_create` takes a device by its display name (the machine name `thread_list` shows), in any case, by its id, or by a unique id prefix. An offline device that shares the name no longer stands in for the online one. When several online devices share the name, the call answers `ambiguous_target`. An unknown name answers `not_found` and lists the account's devices by name and id. (#14072)
