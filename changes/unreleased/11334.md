## 2026-10-26 - Preserve typed abort confirmation and queue clearing (#11334)

Both stdio and hosted runners send abort with an effect identity, awaitIdle and
clearQueue. The hosted recovery path preserves the same identity when reissuing
the command. Interrupt, cancel and task_cancel details carry the host's receipt,
including negative stop confirmations, without inventing stopped or cleared facts
when the receipt is unavailable. Existing foreign-user interruption, local
cancellation and provider-abort classification is unchanged.
