### Fixed

- The `/remote` close in the TUI follows `thread_close`'s close contract (#14234). A close refused because the thread has messages it has not read names `/remote close <session> --force` and lists those messages, or says the ids are unavailable on that device's engine. The picker then offers "Close anyway". A forced close lists the messages it dropped. The refusal's `details.reason` is the engine's own reason (`turn_active` or `work_pending`). ([#14246](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14246), fixes [#14245](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/14245))
