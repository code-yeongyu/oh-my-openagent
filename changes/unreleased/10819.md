### Changed

- Memory transcript journals are bounded: reasoning is cut to a 2,048-character window at capture, journals reflection has consumed are retired after 7 idle days (only per-message dedupe keys stay), and while an identity's transcripts exceed 32 MiB the oldest non-live journals idle for 24 hours are retired too, consumed or not. This runs in the existing 12-hour memory maintenance pass, which also compacts an already oversized store once. `/doctor` adds a `storage` line with bytes per area, and terminal facts runs keep at most 16 MiB (was 128 MiB).
