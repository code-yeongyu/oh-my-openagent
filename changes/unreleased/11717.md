### Added

- Computer use (macOS): the engine can now list the installed and running applications (`apps.list`) and open an application, document or URL without bringing it forward (`apps.open`), returning the window it shows. Opening in the background launches, unhides or reopens the application without activating anything and needs no foreground-control grant; `activate` and `reveal` (show the item selected in Finder) take your foreground and need the human-confirmed grant, like raising a window. An ambiguous application name is refused with the two candidate paths rather than launching either; other platforms refuse with `AxUnsupported`. The `computer.apps.*` helpers that expose this in runs and the prelude follow in the next change. ([#11717](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/11717), part of [#11463](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/11463))
