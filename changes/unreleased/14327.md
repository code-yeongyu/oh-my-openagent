### Added

- The remote client (thread SDK `remote`) accepts a device by its name (any Unicode form, case or inner spacing), `here`, or a unique id prefix of at least 6 characters in `sessions.resolve`, every session member and `attach`; the device part goes through the shared address module's resolver.

### Fixed

- A remote session member no longer sends an unresolved session part to its device: only a thread id, a routing id `rpc-N` or a 22-character listing key passes through unlisted (a session opened a moment ago); any other text no listing resolves answers `not_found` with `details.kind: "session"`.
- A remote miss on an online device that did not answer the listing round answers `host_unavailable` with `details.reason: "listing_incomplete"` naming it, in `sessions.resolve`, the session members and `aliases.resolve` / `set` / `clear`; `device_offline` is only for a device the roster says is offline. A device-qualified miss is never blamed on another device's silence.
