### Fixed

- Telemetry turn spans carry the same closed session role as the events (`root`, `child` or `unknown`) instead of the engine's internal role name. The collector refused those spans, about half of all records in 5.1.54 and 5.1.55. A closed telemetry field now never leaves with a value outside its list: it becomes that list's catch-all or is left out, and session summaries re-mask providers recovered from older windows. ([#12062](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/12062), fixes [#12061](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/12061))
