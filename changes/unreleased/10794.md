### Changed

- Messages between sessions now show the sender's workspace and project first, plus lane and session names when available. Configure `provenance.workspaces` in `omo.json` or set `OMO_PROVENANCE_WORKSPACE` and `OMO_PROVENANCE_PROJECT` in the sender's environment. Unmatched senders remain deliverable as `unclassified`; older stored messages say provenance was not recorded (#10794).
