### Fixed

- `thread_list` no longer reports the sessions of a device that is offline or did not answer as live. Their rows come from the relay's cached snapshot and are now listed `resumable`; opening one with `thread_create` asks that device to reopen it, and answers `device_offline` while the device stays offline. A listed thread with an empty title shows its folder's name instead of an empty name; that label is for display only and is not an address.
