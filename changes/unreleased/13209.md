## 2026-11-12 - model_profile verbs: planned backup, account homes from the user database (#13209)

`project-model-profile-rpc.ts` passes `updateOmoConfig` a planned `backupPath` (`<file>.bak.<stamp>-<uuid>`) and removes exactly that path after the write (or after a failed write, when it exists), replacing the before/after directory listing. `isHomeFolder` compares the folder by device and inode with HOME, the account home, and every home from `/etc/passwd` plus, on macOS, `dscl . -list /Users NFSHomeDirectory` (read once per process; `deps.accountHomes` replaces `deps.accountsRoot`); the sibling-of-home rule and `accountsRootFor` are removed.
