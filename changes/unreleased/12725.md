**A session on another device can now wake you when it changes.** `omo remote watch <device>/<session>` prints the session's status changes and turn ends as they happen, keeps watching through relay drops and device-service restarts, and reports once what changed while it could not see the session. `thread_send` and `thread_create` on another device's session now return `monitor_target`, and with an engine that supports remote monitor targets, `monitor({ type: "rpc", target: "<device>/<session>" })` wakes the agent on that session just as it does on a local one. `omo remote send` now finishes even when its turn ends during a relay drop.
