### Changed

- The built-in deep categories now require one exact model each: GPT-6.2 Astra at max for `ultrabrain`, GPT-6.2 Astra at high for `deep-high`, and plain GPT-6.2 Sol at medium for `deep-low`. Providers can still supply the same model, but an older model or a Fast sibling no longer activates these categories. Explicit model overrides still work. The Codex installer and bundled Astra agents now default to GPT-6.2 Astra. ([#14427](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14427))

## 2026-11-20 - Exact GPT models for deep categories (#14427)
