### Fixed

- A task or Kibitzer recall child pinned to a `-fast` model, such as the builtin `quick` head `gpt-6-luna-fast`, now falls back to the next model in its chain on a usage limit or provider error. senpi runs a paired `-fast` alias on its base model and looks up the chain by that base model, but the chain was registered only under the alias, so it was never found. With the ChatGPT plan at its limit, recall failed on every turn until the session reported `Kibitzer gate failed`. Thanks to @codeg-dev.
