### Fixed
- A session started on an explicitly chosen model (`--model`, or a launch that names its model) now stays on that model: when the model fails with a rate limit or another error the fallback chain would have taken, the turn ends with an error naming the model and the cause instead of continuing on another model. Sessions on the default model keep their fallback chain, and every switch and switch back is now noted to the model. ([#13289](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13289))
