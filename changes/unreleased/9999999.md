### Fixed

- A launcher waiting on its engine child no longer dies when a signal reaches it in the instant after the child starts: SIGUSR1 is now relayed to the child, and SIGTERM, SIGHUP and SIGINT get the usual grace window, from the moment the child exists. Before, the listeners were installed only after the spawn returned, so on a busy machine a supervisor's SIGUSR1 wake-up could kill the launcher and orphan the engine. ([#9999999](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/9999999), fixes [#13979](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/13979))
