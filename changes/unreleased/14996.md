### Added

- The omo SDK answers every account call itself: `account.show`, `account.signInCheck`, `account.signOut`, `account.forget` and `account.store.status`, `enable` and `disable` are new, and `account.signIn` runs the device-code or the browser sign-in in the caller's process from the `omo-ai/sdk` entry, handing the codes to `onUserCode` only. `omo login`, `omo logout`, `omo whoami` and `omo account show|sign-in|forget|store` now print what those calls answer, with unchanged output and exit codes. ([#14996](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14996), fixes [#14364](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/14364))
- `omo logout` and `omo account forget` record each run in an owner-only custody log in the account home (ids, kinds and outcomes only, kept 90 days); neither waits on it. ([#14996](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14996))
