### Changed

- Skills: `ulw-research` and `mass-ulw` now require data first. A conclusion is proven by probing a source, reading the file, or running a tool before it is asserted, never by plausibility, recall, or the shape of the code. The research editions state it in their opening contract and make the success criterion universal instead of limited to claims already flagged as contested; the mass-ulw node prompt contract carries it in its VERIFY item.
