### Changed

- The `visualize`, `data-scientist` and `frontend` skills now teach how to build a chart page shown inline beside a reply. Figures are computed at their source (SQL in the database the data lives in, DuckDB or Polars over files). An inline chart is drawn in HTML instead of a matplotlib PNG, with `:root { color-scheme: light dark; }` so `light-dark()` fallbacks follow the theme. Bars are HTML elements in a fixed-height container, the first and last axis labels are aligned to the chart edges so neither clips at 390 px, days are cut in the reader's time zone with a partial day marked on its own bar, and one figure is cross-checked against a second source. The preview step now measures each chart's largest bar and legend colours in dark and light at the reply width and 390 px. `frontend` exempts an inline page from its `DESIGN.md` gate, Lighthouse audit and research lanes, which `visualize` already assumed.
