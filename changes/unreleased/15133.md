## 2026-11-27 - A DAG node re-watches a child that resumes on its own (#12134)

The scheduler subscribes to a completed node's task through the optional `TaskManager.subscribeSelfResumed` and re-attaches it when the child reopens its turn, so the resumed outcome folds onto the node. A host that does not implement the subscription keeps the previous behaviour. Port of #11387.
