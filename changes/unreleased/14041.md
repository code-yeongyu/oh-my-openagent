### Fixed

- `thread_read`, `thread_send` and the other thread tools no longer hang when a session on another machine never answers. A command waits at most 120 s for its result and a read at most 30 s; past that, a read answers `host_unavailable` (nothing changed there) and a command that may have run answers `idempotency_uncertain`.
