**Memory sync drops a replaced desktop account cleanly.** When the desktop moved to another account without announcing a retirement, the engine opened the new account but left the old one half alive: it kept listening on the desktop channel and its open sync sessions did not end. The old account now ends as soon as the engine sees it is no longer current, which also ends its sessions. In a session Worker, an operation started just before the desktop retired its account can no longer hand back a handle from the old account. Carried by the next train top's bundle regeneration.
