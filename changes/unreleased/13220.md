### Added
- Thread gateway: a message another of your signed-in devices sends to a session that is not running on this device can now be queued here and the session started to take it, through the delivery consumer, the carrier that opens the session in its own working directory and hands it back when its turn is done, and the pending sweep (#13037). A session opens only for a queued message whose own switch allows it: messages from another device need the receiving device's wake switch, and messages from this device need `session_wake.same_device`. Both are off by default, and nothing is started for another device until a device service release carrying that switch is out and the switch is turned on. ([#13220](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13220))
