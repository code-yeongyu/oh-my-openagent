### Fixed

- `deep-low` and `deep-high` no longer finish on the parent session's model from another vendor once every OpenAI rung is usage-limited or signed out. They now fail with `failure_kind: "provider_lock_exhausted"` and an error naming the category and the reset time the provider reported. The new opt-in `categories.<name>.provider_lock` key does this: the two deep categories ship with it on (set `false` to restore the parent-model fallback), and every other category, including your own one-vendor chains, keeps the parent-model fallback unless you set it to `true`. ([#12006](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/12006))
