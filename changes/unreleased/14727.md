### Fixed

- On Windows, cleanup of hook commands, swept processes and terminated process trees no longer uses `taskkill /T`, which could also end an unrelated older process whose recorded parent PID had been recycled. It ends only the owned process and its creation-time-checked descendants by explicit PID, and signals nothing when the process list cannot be read. ([#14727](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14727))
