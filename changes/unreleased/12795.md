### Fixed

- Memory shutdown drain: the session-shutdown wiring no longer claims senpi puts no bound on its handler. senpi bounds one `session_shutdown` handler with its own warn threshold, per-handler timeout and total quit budget (shipped defaults 2 s / 10 s / 2 s, each user-tunable and 0-disableable), and the 1500 ms drain budget is sized under those defaults and pinned by a test that reads them from the installed senpi, so a host release that lowers a default below the drain fails there. No host cap was added from OmO and nothing bypasses the host abort; a journal flush skipped by the budget still raises its error-level alarm. ([#12795](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/12795), refs [#10336](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/10336))
