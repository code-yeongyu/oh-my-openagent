### Fixed

- A delivery queued for a session whose engine waits on its own paused or blocked goal (or a stalled turn) is now delivered: the wake carrier opens the session and sends the engine's wake instead of treating that wait as a person's. Only a person's wait keeps a queued delivery out: a question, an approval, an interrupted run, a pending dialog, or a wait kind omo does not know. The carrier records that wait's kind in `session_wait.hold_kinds`. Delivering does not resume the paused goal; the delivered message runs as its own turn (#15279).
