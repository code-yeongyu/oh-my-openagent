**`omo remote` takes a device by the name its table prints.** The session verbs (`open`, `send`, `model`, `interrupt`, `close`) accept a device's id, its name, or a unique prefix of either, through the same resolver `thread_create` uses. A name or prefix two devices share is refused with `ambiguous_device`, naming each candidate; a device nothing matches is `device_unknown`.
