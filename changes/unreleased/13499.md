**`omo remote` takes a device by the name its table prints.** The session verbs (`open`, `send`, `model`, `interrupt`, `close`) accept a device's id, its name, or a unique prefix of either, through the same resolver `thread_create` uses, and their output names the device by its id. A string that names two devices (two names, a name and a prefix, or an id that is another device's name) is refused with `ambiguous_device`, listing each candidate; a device nothing matches is `device_unknown`. **`thread_create` contract change:** its `device` now also accepts a unique prefix, and a device it cannot find is answered `device_unknown` (it was `not_found`); an ambiguous one is `ambiguous_device`.
