**Each delegated child shows the model it actually used and why it fell back.** Task status, `/tasks`, and the Agents side panel name the actual provider and model. A child that moves off a limited provider keeps the provider's explanation and shows a reset instant only when the pinned engine reports one. Ordinary rate limits are not labeled as spent accounts, and a later failure does not reuse an earlier turn's reason. Categories keep their own model choices.
