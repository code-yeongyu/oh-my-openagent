**Background task limits can be turned off.** `background_task.staleTimeoutMs`, `messageStalenessTimeoutMs`, `taskTtlMs` and `syncPollTimeoutMs` accept `0` for never, so a long-running child is no longer cancelled while it works quietly, and the new `syncMaxAssistantTurns` (default 300, `0` for no cap) replaces the fixed turn cap of a foreground task. Defaults are unchanged. ([#13168](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13168))
