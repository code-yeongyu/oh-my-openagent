**A model chain that runs one model at two thinking levels keeps both rungs, and the run is labelled with the level it actually uses.** When a category or an agent listed the same model twice at different reasoning levels - "try it thinking, then retry it with thinking off" - the second entry and every entry after it vanished from the chain, and the run was recorded with the level of the LAST entry for that model. Rungs are now identified by their model AND their thinking level: both entries survive, the selected rung keeps its own level, and the task record's remaining-rung and fallback-attempt bookkeeping trims at the level in use.
