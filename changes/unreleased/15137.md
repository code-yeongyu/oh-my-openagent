### Fixed

- A child task whose turn ends while its own background work is still running (a detached eval cell, a background command, a monitor, its own children) is no longer recorded as completed with its progress note as the result: the turn is held until the turn that work wakes answers. Work that ends without waking the child, or that stays silent for an hour, ends the task as `async_work_pending`, never with the progress note (#10487).

## 2026-11-27 - A completed turn is held while the child's own async work is pending (#10487)

Every runner asks the child for its pending async work before settling a turn as completed and holds the turn while any is live; the held progress note is never the result: the turn the work wakes supersedes it, and work that drains with no new turn, or makes no progress within the bound, ends it as `async_work_pending`. Port of #12133.
