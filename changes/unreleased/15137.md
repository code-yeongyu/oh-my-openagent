### Fixed

- A child task whose turn ends while its own background work is still running (a detached eval cell, a background command, a monitor, its own children) is no longer recorded as completed with its progress note as the result: the turn is held until the turn that work wakes answers. Work that ends without waking the child, or that stays silent for an hour, ends the task as `async_work_pending`, never with the progress note. This also holds when the session ends before the turn's idle edge, and after the parent restarts, restores an in-process child, or the host reopens the child's session from its file (#10487). Cancelling a workflow or a task whose child is waiting for its own background work ends that child at once; no later turn runs.

## 2026-11-27 - A completed turn is held while the child's own async work is pending (#10487)

Every runner asks the child for its pending async work before settling a turn as completed and holds the turn while any is live; the held progress note is never the result: the turn the work wakes supersedes it, and work that drains with no new turn, or makes no progress within the bound, ends it as `async_work_pending`. A reopened or respawned session reads the same fact from the engine's `session-lifecycle` records in the session file, and a host park persists that outcome before it parks the record. A cancel ends an in-process child's held turn as `cancelled` in the same call (the handle's `markStopping`), so the session is destroyed at once and its work wakes no turn after the cancel; a wake turn already running ends `cancelled` and is never held again. Port of #12133.
