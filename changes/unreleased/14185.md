### Fixed

- `thread_send` and `thread_read` to a session on another device no longer wait past what an eval tool call allows: the command waits, wakes and attaches of one call now share one 50 s budget, so the call answers a typed result instead of being dropped untyped. A message the relay accepted for the other device but whose answer was lost is reported as the new `delivered_unconfirmed` (it may have run: read the thread before sending again).
