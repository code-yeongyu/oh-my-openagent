### Fixed

- `thread_send`, `thread_read` and `thread_create` aimed at another device no longer wait past what an eval tool call allows: the command waits, wakes, creates and attaches of one call now share one 50 s budget, and a `thread_read` wait on another device leaves the read after it enough time to return the transcript. A message the relay accepted for the other device but whose answer was lost is reported as the new `delivered_unconfirmed` (it may have run: read the thread before sending again).
