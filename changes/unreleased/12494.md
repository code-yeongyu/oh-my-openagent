### Fixed

- The browser skill's docs and examples teach BrowserSkill 0.4 shared control for the omowright it now ships (`^0.3.0`): every gated page action carries the context of a fresh `session.cooperate("sync")`, and a `cooperation_sync_required` refusal waits for the person with `session.cooperate("wait")`, then re-syncs and replans. They also note which calls are gated, that a gated action throws a `TypeError` on an unknown option key (with the two calls that pass stray keys through instead), and that `page.on("dialog")` listeners receive a `Dialog` they can answer. The omowright this release ships includes running-browser discovery through `DevToolsActivePort`, `goto` on sparse and frameset pages, UTF-8 decoding across BrowserSkill IPC chunks and unique-only stale-ref recovery (thanks @ashmoonori-afk, code-yeongyu/omowright#115 to #118), and agent tabs in their own window (thanks @devxoul, code-yeongyu/omowright#120).
