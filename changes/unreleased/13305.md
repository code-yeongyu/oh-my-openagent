**Text from another device prints escaped in the thread tools' output.** A thread title, project path or refusal reason that another device sends is shown with its control, format and line-separator characters escaped (`\u202e`, `\u001b`, ...) wherever `thread_list`, `thread_read`, `thread_create` and the control tools print it, including the candidates of an ambiguous name; nothing is deleted, and addressing a thread by its exact title still finds it. ([#13305](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13305), [#13304](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/13304))
