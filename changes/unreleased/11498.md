**Every omo.dev page now declares its own language and the dark colour scheme, and starts with a skip link.** Korean, Japanese, Chinese and the other locales set `<html lang>` to their language (it was always English), form controls and scrollbars render dark, the first Tab offers "Skip to content", focused controls no longer hide under the sticky header, and unknown pages show a 404 in the reader's language.
