## 2026-10-30 - Engine errors carry a user-facing notice contract (senpi#4990)

`ComputerEngineUnavailableError` (`components/computer-use/engine-status.ts`) and the engine's `DesktopLaunchContextError`, `DesktopEngineAbiMismatchError` and `DesktopEngineHandshakeError` carry an own `userFacing` property `{ code, message, action }` (`UserFacingErrorInfo`, exported by `@oh-my-opencode/senpi-desktop-engine`). Senpi recognizes it structurally and renders it as a calm notice, writing the stack to the debug log only; an older senpi ignores the field. Codes: `COMPUTER_LAUNCH_CONTEXT_REQUIRED`, `COMPUTER_ENGINE_VERSION_MISMATCH`, `COMPUTER_ENGINE_START_FAILED`, `COMPUTER_ENGINE_QUARANTINED`, `COMPUTER_ENGINE_UNAVAILABLE`, `COMPUTER_UNSUPPORTED_PLATFORM`. A wrapped ABI or launch-context error forwards its own contract. No user-facing text names a file or engine path.
