### Fixed

- A self-hosted or otherwise unrecognized openai-compatible model no longer loses its reasoning effort: when the model's own `variants` declare a `reasoningEffort`, the requested variant's effort now reaches the provider instead of being stripped as an unknown model family. Recognized models keep their known effort ladder. ([#12181](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/12181), fixes [#9140](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/9140))
