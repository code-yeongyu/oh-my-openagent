**A monthly facts note no longer grows without bound between memory dreams.** When a committed `notes/facts/<YYYY-MM>.md` passes `memory.facts_note_max_bytes` (48 KiB by default, `0` turns it off), OmO starts the same background pressure dream it uses for an oversized `system/`, and that dream is told which notes are over budget: it retires superseded and day-only facts and merges duplicates, summarizing the rest into `ARCHIVE.md`, without dropping a fact that still applies. `/doctor` lists the notes over the budget with their size. ([#10884](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/10884), [#12933](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/12933))
