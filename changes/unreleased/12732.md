## 2026-11-07 - thread_create keeps a thread's own alias when reopening it (#12641)

`components/thread/remote/plan-create.ts` `planCreate` treats an alias whose holder is the thread being opened (`thread` is the alias, the holder's id, or any address the create's resolver locates to the holder, such as its relay key) as not taken, and locates that thread once for both the check and the open; `withAlias` then confirms the holder's existing claim. Any other holder is still `alias_taken` before anything is opened. Items 1-4 of #12641 are not part of this change.
