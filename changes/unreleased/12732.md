## 2026-11-07 - thread_create keeps a thread's own alias when reopening it (#12641)

`components/thread/remote/cross-device.ts` `planCreate` treats an alias whose holder is the thread being opened (`thread` equals the alias, or equals the holder's `session_id`) as not taken; `withAlias` then confirms the holder's existing claim. Any other holder is still `alias_taken` before anything is opened. Items 1-4 of #12641 are not part of this change.
