**Session locks can be handed to a child process for one call.** `withLock` now gives its callback a lease: `handOver(pid)` makes a child the lock's live holder and `restore()` takes it back, aborting the critical section if the lock changed meanwhile. The macOS keychain helper uses it so no other writer can enter while a keychain change is landing.
