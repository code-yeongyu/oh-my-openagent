**Following a session on another device no longer loses what was written during a reconnect.** When the connection to the device service drops while `omo remote watch`, `omo remote send` or a monitored remote session is following, the follow now resumes from the last record it applied and gets exactly the records written meanwhile, none twice, instead of skipping them and resyncing from a fresh state read. This needs a relay daemon that supports resuming an attach; with an older one the follow resyncs as before. ([#12771](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/12771))
