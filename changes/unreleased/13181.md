**A foreground `call_omo_agent` follows the wait settings.** Its wait for the child is bounded by `background_task.syncPollTimeoutMs` (still 5 minutes when unset) instead of a fixed 5 minutes, and with `foregroundHandoffMs` set a child still running after that long moves to the background like a foreground `task`: the call returns at once and the child's answer arrives in its notice.
