**Plans and executors stop spending the deep categories on mechanical work, and verifiers get them instead.** ulw-plan now defaults implementation todos to `quick` / `unspecified-*` and requires a stated reason for `deep-*` or `ultrabrain`; final-verifier rows route to `deep-high` (`deep-low` for checks that only run fixed commands). ulw-execute and mass-ulw name the same verifier route, and a retry, "finish" continuation or repair of checkpoint findings keeps the category of the todo it repairs, climbing at most one rung with a recorded reason. Builds, installs and device or emulator steps stay at or below `unspecified-high`.
