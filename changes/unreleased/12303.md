### Fixed

- A device whose memory-sync sign-in ends (revoked, signed out, `omo logout`, `omo account forget --device`, or replaced by a new sign-in) no longer keeps its sync trust pin, so `omo account show --account` stops reporting it as joined and re-pairing after a reset is no longer blocked. Joining again always goes through the pairing or recovery ceremony. The new `omo account retire-sync --account <id>` does the same for a desktop-owned session. ([#12280](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/12280))
