### Added
- `bun run qa:rpc-relay-parity` measures the engine's RPC methods on a pair of signed-in devices: the same record locally and through the relay, compared and classified `same`, `differs` or `missing`. It probes 113 of the engine's 128 methods: the 15 `connection_local` methods are excluded by design, and 36 destructive or credential methods are probed only in a harmless form the engine must refuse. The local-versus-relay parity table records the measured verdict for each of the 113. ([#13895](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13895))
