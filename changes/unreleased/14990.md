### Fixed

- `omo remote model`, `omo remote interrupt` and `omo remote close` no longer command a session its device lists as ended: they answer as the thread tools do (`not_resumable` for model and interrupt, `closed: false` with reason `not_live` for close) and send nothing to that device. ([#13016](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/13016))
- A fuzzy `thread_handoff` to an address the exact lookup finds ambiguous answers `ambiguous_target` with its candidates instead of `not_found`. ([#13016](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/13016))
- `ThreadSdk.send` to a thread on another device answers the `delivery_id` of the prompt it sent, as `thread_send` answers its delivery's. ([#13016](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/13016))

### Changed

- The thread tools, `ThreadSdk`, the monitor target and `omo remote` resolve a thread address through one shared function with one policy (replies, the reply grant, workspace scope, `self`, text with a slash). Answers are unchanged. ([#13016](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/13016))
