### Added

- git-master commit attribution can be turned off with `git config omo.attribution false` (or `sisyphus.attribution`) or with `NO_AI_ATTRIBUTION=1`, `OMO_NO_COMMIT_ATTRIBUTION=1` or `OMO_GIT_ATTRIBUTION=0`, which drops both the footer and the co-author trailer. `omo.attribution true` or `OMO_GIT_ATTRIBUTION=1` turns the builtin footer on and leaves `include_co_authored_by` as configured. ([#7833](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/7833))
