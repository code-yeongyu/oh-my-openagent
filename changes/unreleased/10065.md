**Atlas no longer repeats its final summary after a plan is finished.** The "plan complete" reminder is meant to reach Atlas once, but a finished session that sat idle for 10 minutes, or survived a restart, got it again on every later idle, so Atlas kept reprinting the same ORCHESTRATION COMPLETE report. The reminder is now recorded with the plan's work state, so it is sent once per plan. ([#4744](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/4744), [#10065](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/10065))
