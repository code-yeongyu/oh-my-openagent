## 2026-11-16 - Settle failed retry and continuation recovery (#13805, #13785)

The shared RPC turn observer consumes `auto_retry_end(success:false)` and `continuation_error` through its existing held-error confirmation. It preserves the original retry error when the final event omits it, honors user cancellation, and clears the held error when another `agent_start` takes over. Both process and host-session children use this observer.

The regressions cover a declined retry with no later end or idle event, a continuation rejection, a successful retry, and a real continuation superseding a held failure. The upstream local-compaction fallback classifier is corrected separately in senpi#6833.
