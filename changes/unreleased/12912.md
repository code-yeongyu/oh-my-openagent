**The thread tools are eval-only, like `bash`.** Whenever the `eval` tool is available, `thread_create`, `thread_list`, `thread_read`, `thread_send`, `thread_interrupt`, `thread_close`, `thread_handoff`, `thread_rename`, `thread_set_model`, `thread_set_reasoning`, `thread_bind`, `thread_unbind`, `thread_rebind`, `thread_bindings`, `thread_report`, `thread_outbox`, `thread_outbox_ack` and `thread_answer` leave the model's direct tool list and are called as `tool.thread_send({ ... })` inside an eval cell (`tool_schema("thread_send")` lists the parameters); the system prompt names the eval form of every one of them and a direct call answers with that pointer instead of running. Without an eval tool they stay ordinary direct tools. Parameters, permissions, results and the family guideline are unchanged. ([#12912](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/12912))
