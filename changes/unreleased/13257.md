### Added

- `omo remote wake [on|off|default]` reads or sets whether sessions on this device that are not running may be started by a message from your other signed-in devices; `default` follows the build's default, which is off. Until a device service that carries this switch is released, the command sends nothing and says so. ([#13257](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13257), part of [#13037](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/13037))
