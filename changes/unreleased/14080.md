### Added

- `omo account devices` lists your account's devices with their ids, and `omo account devices revoke <id>` signs out another of your devices by id, for example the old registration a computer left behind when it signed in again. It refuses this device's own id (use `omo logout`) and any id that is not one of your live devices, and reports success only after the account no longer lists the device. `omo account forget --device` naming another device now points to this command.
