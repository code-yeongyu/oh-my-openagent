## 2026-11-27 - A child whose revival is pending is neither orphaned nor held (#15150)

`TaskLifecycle.pendingRevivals` (`lifecycle/revival-pending.ts`) answers whether this host still owes a child a revival: live-parent recovery's own eligibility (`awaitsLiveParentRecovery`, now shared with `live-parent-recovery.ts`) on the fresh record, bounded by the `recovery_deadline_at` it persists; `changed()` settles after every live-parent pass. DAG recovery judges a non-terminal child with `childBacking` (`dag/child-backing.ts`): live, revival pending, or orphaned (#8657); never-launched children keep #12107 re-admission. A pending node keeps its state and goes to its scheduler as `awaitingRevival` (`dag/scheduler-pending-revival.ts`): it sits in the settle loop so its dependents wait and the run never parks, follows the child once it has a handle, folds a terminal record, and fails `resume_task_orphaned` once the lifecycle no longer owes the revival.
