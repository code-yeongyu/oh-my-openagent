## 2026-11-27 - A child whose revival is pending is neither orphaned nor held (#15150)

`TaskLifecycle.pendingRevivals` (`lifecycle/revival-pending.ts`) answers live-parent recovery's own revival obligation for a child (`obligation(taskId)`: `owed` while an attempt or expiry for it is in flight or it is still eligible - the final attempt past `recovery_deadline_at` included -, `none` once the lifecycle has no obligation, `stopped` once the lifecycle is disposed or the session's retries stop); `changed()` settles after every live-parent pass, at the end of every expiry, on dispose and when retries stop. `expiryInFlight` (`lifecycle/suspended-expiry.ts`) marks an expiry for its whole run. DAG recovery judges a non-terminal child with `childBacking` (`dag/child-backing.ts`): live, revival pending, or orphaned (#8657); never-launched children keep #12107 re-admission. A pending node keeps its state and goes to its scheduler as `awaitingRevival` (`dag/scheduler-pending-revival.ts`): it sits in the settle loop so its dependents wait and the run never parks, follows the child once it has a handle, folds a terminal record (a child the lifecycle gave up on folds there too when recovery finds it already ended, so the run's infrastructure retry applies either way), fails `resume_task_orphaned` once the lifecycle has no obligation, and is released without a journal write (the run parks) when the lifecycle stops; it keeps no clock of its own.
