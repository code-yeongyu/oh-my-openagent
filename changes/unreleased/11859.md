**Reopening a session after a mid-turn quit resumes its background tasks again.** Opening a session whose last run was interrupted installs an engine hold that waits for your next message; the task runtime read that hold as a user Stop, paused every child parked by the quit instead of reviving it, refused new `task` spawns as "held by your Stop", and left the widget on `resuming (session quit)` for good. Only a real Stop now pauses the run; a plain reopen revives the parked children through the session-start reconcile.
