## 2026-11-22 - Distinguish an interrupted reopen from an explicit Stop

`packages/omo-senpi/src/components/task/engine-hold-reader.ts` excludes the engine's `interrupted-run` session hold from the user-Stop check while retaining real Stop holds and run fences. Session-start reconciliation can then revive the parked child without creating a pause ledger.

## 2026-11-22 - Engine lifecycle rows do not hide an interrupted turn

`packages/senpi-task/src/manager/interrupted-turn.ts` treats the engine's lifecycle ledger rows (`session-lifecycle`, `work-custody`, `child-lifecycle`) as bookkeeping. A graceful quit appends them after the aborted turn, so the continuation check stopped at them and the revived child never received its continuation prompt.
