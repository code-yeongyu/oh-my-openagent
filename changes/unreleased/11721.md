### Added

- Computer use (macOS): the session now stops when you step into the app it is driving. Input aimed at an app you have used since the session last looked at its window waits for you to pause, then fails `UserIntervened` without sending anything; input already under way stops at your first key press or click in that app and says what may have landed; a window read (screenshot, observe, AX snapshot or query) that begins after a 5 s pause clears it.
