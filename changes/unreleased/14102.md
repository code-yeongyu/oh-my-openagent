### Fixed

- On macOS, `omo logout` and `omo account forget` can delete the saved sign-in again. Every launch runs a fresh copy of the keychain helper, and removing the item from any copy other than the one that created it failed with `-25244`. The helper now deletes the exact item it found. Items owned by another app are still never touched. ([#14102](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/14102))
