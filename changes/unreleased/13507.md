## 2026-11-14 - `omo remote interrupt` names the background jobs it stopped (#13444 review follow-up)

`remote-render.ts` `sessionVerbLines`: an interrupt that stopped a running turn adds one line listing the stopped background jobs (id and description) and one line per job it could not stop (id and reason) from the result's `background`; `background.status: "not_supported"` adds a line saying the jobs were left running. "Nothing was running" stays one line. The `--json` output is unchanged.
