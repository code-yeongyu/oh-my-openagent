### Changed

- Memory sync: once a device has synced on 5.1.64 or later, going back to an older release stops its pushes. The older release still records your edits, but it cannot read the pending-change format 5.1.64 introduced, so every push attempt fails and nothing is sent until you upgrade again; the queued edits are kept and go out then (#12506). ([#13661](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13661))
