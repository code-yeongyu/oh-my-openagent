### Fixed

- Memory sync: an `omo logout` that could not get the service to confirm the device revoke (offline, a server error, an uncertain refresh) no longer leaves that device's sync key in the account keyring with nothing left to remove it. Logout still tells you to remove the device on the Devices page of your OmO account; in addition, the revoke is now owed, and a later `omo login` on the same computer that the service accepts sends it with the new sign-in, after which a remaining member rotates the old key out. While the service still counts the old device as this computer's own, that sign-in is refused, so the Devices page stays the way to remove it. An owe is sent only for a device the signed-in account lists as this computer's kind of device, never at the cost of the sign-in: a revoke it cannot send is reported and tried again at the next sign-in (#12281).
