### Added

- `thread_list` rows of a held thread carry the engine's `activity` (`idle`, `running`, `awaiting_approval`, `queued` or `errored`), `error_code` with `errored`, and `turn_id` when the engine reports them (senpi#6688). `state` folds them: `queued` and `awaiting_approval` are `running`, `errored` is `idle`; inactive and archived rows carry none. ([#13504](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/13504))
